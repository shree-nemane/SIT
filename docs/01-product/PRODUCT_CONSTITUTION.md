# Stay in Touch

## Product Constitution

Version: 1.0 (Draft)
Status: Accepted

---

# Vision

**Stay in Touch** is a private application that helps close friends remain connected after graduation by sharing a simple, living snapshot of their current lives.

The goal is not to increase communication.

The goal is to reduce the distance created by time, work, and geography.

---

# Problem

After graduation, friendships rarely end because people stop caring.

They fade because life becomes busy.

Everyone moves to different cities, jobs, universities, and schedules.

Keeping up with everyone becomes difficult.

Eventually, the question

> "What have you been up to?"

becomes harder to answer than it should be.

---

# Solution

Every friend maintains a single current update consisting of:

- One photo
- One short description
- Last updated time

Together these updates create a living snapshot of the group.

Opening the application—or simply glancing at the home screen widget—should immediately answer:

> "What is everyone up to these days?"

---

# Core Principles

## Private by Design

Built only for close friends.

No public profiles.

No strangers.

No discovery.

---

## One Living Update

Each person has one active update.

Updating replaces the previous one.

The focus is always on the present.

---

## Effortless

Updating should take less than one minute.

If it feels like posting on social media, the design has failed.

---

## Glanceable

The application should provide value without being opened.

The home screen widget is a core part of the product.

---

## Authentic

Encourage genuine life updates.

The objective is awareness, not presentation.

---

## Ambient Connection

The application should quietly exist in everyday life.

A glance at the widget should be enough to know how friends are doing.

No endless scrolling.

No unnecessary notifications.

No engagement mechanics.

---

# Success Criteria

The product succeeds when friends naturally know what is happening in each other's lives without needing to ask.

Connection is the metric.

Engagement is not.

---

# Non Goals

This application is not:

- A social media platform
- A messaging application
- A photo sharing platform
- A content platform
- A productivity application

---

# Design Philosophy

Every decision should answer one question.

> Does this make it easier for close friends to stay in touch?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in the product.
