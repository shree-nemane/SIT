# Stay in Touch

## Why This Product Exists

---

## The Beginning

Friendships don't usually end after graduation.

They slowly fade.

Not because people stop caring.

But because life begins.

One friend moves to another city.

Someone starts their first job.

Someone prepares for higher studies.

Someone gets busy building a career.

Over time, conversations become less frequent.

The question,

> "What have you been up to?"

becomes harder to answer.

Eventually everyone assumes everyone else is simply busy.

---

## The Problem

Today's communication tools are designed for conversations.

Social media is designed for audiences.

Neither is designed for quiet, lifelong friendships.

Messaging applications require someone to start a conversation.

Social media encourages content creation, validation, and engagement.

But most of the time, we don't need a conversation.

We simply want to know that our friends are doing well.

---

## The Opportunity

Imagine opening your phone and, without doing anything, seeing that:

- Aman started his first job.
- Priya moved to Bangalore.
- Rohit is travelling home for the weekend.
- Neha is preparing for interviews.

No messages.

No notifications.

No endless scrolling.

Just a simple reminder that the people you care about are continuing their journey.

That small moment reduces the distance created by time and geography.

---

## Our Belief

We believe staying connected should require less effort than staying disconnected.

Friendships should not depend on remembering to send messages.

Technology should quietly support relationships instead of demanding attention.

---

## Why a Widget?

The widget is not an extra feature.

It is the heart of the product.

Opening an application is an intentional action.

Seeing a widget is effortless.

The product succeeds when users stay connected without remembering to open the app.

---

## Why One Current State?

Most social platforms encourage creating more content.

Stay in Touch encourages maintaining one honest snapshot of life.

There is no pressure to post often.

There is no history to curate.

There is only one simple question:

> "What does your life look like right now?"

---

## Why Private?

Real friendships do not need audiences.

This application is built for people who already know each other.

There are no followers.

There are no strangers.

There is no public discovery.

Privacy is not a feature.

It is part of the product's identity.

---

## What We Refuse To Become

Stay in Touch will never become a platform that competes for attention.

It will never optimize for:

- Screen time
- Engagement
- Virality
- Endless scrolling
- Algorithmic feeds

Those goals belong to social media.

They do not belong here.

---

## What Success Looks Like

Success is not measured by downloads.

Success is not measured by daily active users.

Success is when, years after graduation, a group of friends still knows what is happening in each other's lives.

Success is when someone smiles after glancing at their home screen because they noticed a friend reached a milestone.

Success is when distance no longer feels like absence.

---

## The Promise

Every decision made during the development of Stay in Touch must support one simple promise:

> Help close friends remain part of each other's everyday lives without demanding their time or attention.

If a feature does not strengthen that promise, it does not belong in this product.

---

## Project Motto

**Simple enough to disappear.**

**Meaningful enough to stay.**
